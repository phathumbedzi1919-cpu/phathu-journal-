Why Having A Journal Is Important

Your mind forgets. Your journal doesn't.

1. You never lose your ideas. The idea you have at night can change your life in the morning - if you wrote it down.

2. You see how far you've come. When life feels heavy, you can read back and see: I survived that day too. I am stronger than I thought.

3. You think clearly. When you write, your head becomes quiet. You stop overthinking. You know what to do next.

4. You heal in private. Some things you can't tell anyone. You can tell your journal. No judgment. No gossip. Just you.

5. You stay set on your goal. If you write your goal every day, you will not forget it. And if a person is set to achieve something, they will.

Your journal is your memory. Your memory is your power.
